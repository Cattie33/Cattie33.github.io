This is a **sample post** showing one way to organize reading notes. Replace it with notes on a book you have read when you are ready.

## Begin with a question

Instead of recording every page, start with one question: why am I reading this book? After finishing it, has that question changed?

## Leave your own response

Excerpts are clues; your responses make the notes yours. Even a few sentences can remind your future self what moved you at the time.
